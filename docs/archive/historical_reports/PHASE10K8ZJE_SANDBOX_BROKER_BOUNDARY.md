# PHASE10K8ZJE Sandbox Broker Boundary

## Scope
- `src.brokerage.sandbox` describes a sandbox broker boundary as metadata only.
- It does not enable account creation, order submission, or live trading.

## Ownership
- Sandbox descriptors and status snapshots are local descriptors for future activation planning.
- The sandbox boundary remains disabled and import-safe.

## Status
- Sandbox capabilities remain disabled by default.
- Credentials are not required for import.
- No SDK, network, or broker behavior is activated.
