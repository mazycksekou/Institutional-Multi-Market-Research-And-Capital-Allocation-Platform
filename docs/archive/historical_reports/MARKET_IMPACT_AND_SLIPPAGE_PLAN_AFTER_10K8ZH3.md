# Market Impact and Slippage Plan (After 10K8ZH3)

## Concepts

- **Market impact** – price movement caused by the order itself.
- **Slippage** – difference between expected fill price and actual fill price.
- **Liquidity‑adjusted Kelly** – Kelly stake reduced by estimated impact cost.
- **Order splitting** – slice large orders to reduce market impact.
