# Market Intelligence Standard Report Contract After 10K8ZL2

The standard report contract includes:
- `market`
- `symbol_or_event`
- `current_price_or_odds`
- `bias`
- `confidence`
- `primary_target`
- `secondary_target`
- `stretch_target`
- `expected_move`
- `support`
- `resistance`
- `liquidity_zones`
- `positioning_summary`
- `flow_summary`
- `catalysts`
- `trade_plan`
- `risk`
- `stop`
- `invalidation`
- `reasoning`
- `no_trade_reason`

This contract is deterministic and local-only.
